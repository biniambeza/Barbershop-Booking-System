English (en-US) is the default language. Add Amharic with **Manage translations > Add your own translations > Amharic (am)** and paste the Amharic blocks there.

### Short description (79 / 80)

```
Book a barber in Addis Ababa: pick a shop, a barber and a free time in seconds.
```

### Full description

```
Crown & Blade is the easy way to book a haircut in Addis Ababa.

Find a barbershop near you, see its barbers, prices and reviews, and book a free time in a few taps. No phone calls, no waiting in line.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Browse barbershops by sub-city (Bole, Kazanchis, Piassa, Sarbet, CMC and more) or sort them by distance with "Near me".
• Every shop shows a nearby landmark, the address and a map pin, with Directions to get there.
• See each barber's rating, reviews and haircut photos ("See their work").
• Pick a service, a barber and a day, and see only the times that are really free.
• Reschedule or cancel within the shop's policy.
• Save favourite shops and barbers and book again with one tap.
• Join a shop's walk-in queue from the app and see your place.
• Rate your visit after the haircut and edit your review later.
• English and Amharic.

FOR BARBERS
• Today's bookings and upcoming appointments in one place.
• Accept or decline booking requests, finish a haircut early, mark no-shows.
• Block time off or a whole vacation; booked customers are told.
• Your own haircut portfolio and your earnings for any month.

FOR SHOP OWNERS
• Your shop's profile: photos, services and prices, opening hours, landmark and map pin.
• Add barbers with their own login.
• All bookings of the shop, the walk-in queue and the booking policy (cancellation cutoff, reschedule limit, approve each booking).
• Reports: revenue, best services and barbers, busiest hours, clients and no-show rates.
• Barber commissions, payouts and refunds due.
• Pay your monthly listing by telebirr or CBE transfer and upload the receipt.

Own a barbershop in Addis Ababa? Apply to list it right from the app.

Privacy: we never sell your data and the app has no ads. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
```

## What the listing deliberately leaves out

The project runs on the free Firebase Spark plan, so these features are **not** promised in the text above (Play rejects listings that describe features the app doesn't have):

- Push notifications and booking reminders on the phone, SMS (AfroMessage), Chapa online payment, 6-digit email codes. They need the Blaze plan (Cloud Functions).

After moving to Blaze and deploying the functions, you can add for example "Booking reminders by notification and SMS" and "Pay online with Chapa" to the customer list (and the Amharic "በማሳወቂያና በኤስኤምኤስ የቀጠሮ ማስታወሻዎች", "በChapa በመስመር ላይ ይክፈሉ").
