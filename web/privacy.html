Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-16

Who we are

Crown & Blade is an app for booking barbershops in Addis Ababa. Customers book and pay for haircuts; shop owners and barbers manage their shop, bookings and walk-in queue. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Questions: privacy@example.com

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, contact phone number, app language and role (customer, barber, shop owner or admin). If you sign in with Google we receive your Google email and name.
  • Bookings: the shop, barber, service, date and time, price, status, and reasons you or the shop give when a booking is cancelled or declined.
  • Reviews: the stars and the comment you write. Only your first name is shown with a review.
  • Payments: payment status and payment references (for example a Chapa reference, or the telebirr or CBE transaction number a shop owner enters for a subscription). We never see your card or wallet PIN.
  • Receipt photos: shop owners who pay a subscription by transfer may upload a photo of the receipt.
  • Location: only when a shop owner places the shop pin on the map (the shop location is public), or when you tap "Near me" to sort shops by distance. Your own location is used on your phone to work out distances and is not saved on our servers.
  • Walk-in queue: the name and phone number used to join a shop's queue.
  • Portfolio photos: barbers and shop owners may upload photos of haircuts the barber has done (up to 12 per barber, with an optional style name). These photos are public in the app and on the barber's profile.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send booking reminders, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics: device model, system and app version and what went wrong, no names or messages), and App Check signals (Play Integrity, App Attest or reCAPTCHA) that tell us the request comes from the real app.
  • Favourites are kept on your device only.

How we use it

  • To make, change and cancel bookings and show them to the shop and barber you booked.
  • To send reminders and updates in the app, by push notification and by SMS.
  • To take payments, flag and record refunds, and check shop subscription receipts.
  • To keep the service safe (stop abuse and fake requests) and to fix crashes.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • The shop and barber you book see your name, phone number and booking.
  • Shop profiles, barber profiles and reviews (with the reviewer's first name) are public in the app.
  • Portfolio photos of haircuts are public. A barber must ask the customer before taking or uploading a photo in which the customer can be recognised, and removes it when the customer asks (the barber or the shop owner can delete any photo in the app, or email privacy@example.com and we remove it).
  • A shop's address, nearby landmark and map pin are public so customers can find it.
  • A barber's private phone is shown only to their own customers with a confirmed booking.
  • Crown & Blade administrators can see accounts and shops to run the service; they do not see customers' bookings lists.

Services we use

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and App Check) stores and processes the data on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside Ethiopia. Online payments go through Chapa, SMS messages through AfroMessage, and sign-up codes are sent by email. Google sign-in is provided by Google. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information is kept while your account exists.
  • Bookings are the shop's business records. When you delete your account they stay without your name and phone ("Former customer"). If a refund is still owed to you, the phone number stays on that booking until the shop has paid it back.
  • Reviews: the stars stay as "Former customer"; your comment is deleted.
  • Payment, refund and subscription records are kept as long as needed for accounting and the law.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Crashlytics after 90 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: open the menu (⋮) and tap "Delete account". You type DELETE and sign in again; the app cancels your upcoming bookings (paid ones are flagged for a refund), anonymises your reviews and bookings, deletes your profile, inbox and invitations, and then your login.

  • A barber is also removed from their shop, with their portfolio photos.
  • A shop owner first asks us to remove the shop (the app sends the request), then deletes the account.

Without the app: see https://biniambeza.github.io/Barbershop-Booking-System/delete-account.html or email privacy@example.com from the address you sign in with. We reply within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Change your name, phone number and language in the app at any time.
  • Location is optional: you can refuse the permission and still use the app.
  • You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.

Children

Crown & Blade is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by security rules: each person reads only what their role allows. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you notice a problem.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app changes what it collects, and show the date below.

Email: privacy@example.com
